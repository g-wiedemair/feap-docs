# Potential Fields

Beyond structural mechanics, FEAP is capable of solving potential problems such as **heat distribution** and **seepage flow** [21, 22].

## Governing Equations
Potential problems are reduced to first-order differential equations [23]. The transfer rate $q$ of a physical quantity is defined by the gradient of the potential field $\Phi$ [22]:
$$q = -k\nabla\Phi$$

## Applications
* **Stationary and Transient Analysis**: FEAP handles both stable states and time-dependent changes in temperature or fluid pressure [21].
* **3-Phase Elements**: Advanced modeling for soil mechanics, capturing the displacement of groundwater by compressed air, specifically for tunneling applications [24].
* **Boundary Conditions**: Supports convection and radiation coefficients for realistic thermal modeling [22, 25].
